What is SEO and Why Does It Matter?

Introduction to SEO

SEO, or Search Engine Optimization, is the process of improving your website's visibility on search engines like Google. It helps drive organic, unpaid traffic to your site.

Why SEO is Important

Most users don’t look past the first page of search results. Good SEO ensures your business appears in front of potential customers when they’re searching for what you offer.

Key Takeaways

  • SEO is crucial for online success
  • Organic traffic is valuable and sustainable
  • SEO builds credibility and trust with audiences

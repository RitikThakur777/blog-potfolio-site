About Me

A student of
Markets and Life.

I am Ritik, currently pursuing an MBA in Marketing and Finance, though the journey is less about degrees and more about learning how the world moves. Markets, consumer behavior, and business decisions are not just academic subjects—they are reflections of human choices, incentives, and patterns that quietly shape everyday life. Understanding them feels less like studying a discipline and more like observing a living system.

Learning, however, rarely stays within classrooms. Much of it unfolds while traveling to unfamiliar places, in conversations with different people, or through the discipline of physical activity. These experiences reveal perspectives that no textbook can fully capture, slowly shaping how challenges, opportunities, and ideas are understood.

This space gathers reflections from that ongoing journey—notes on markets, observations about people, and thoughts that emerge somewhere between movement, curiosity, and quiet reflection.
